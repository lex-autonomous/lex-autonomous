MECHANISM: clearly named and explained, logically defensible, differentiated from what buyer has tried
EMOTIONAL SEQUENCING: arc moves pain → possibility → proof → decision, peaks are earned not manufactured
OBJECTION HANDLING: all major objections addressed before they cause disengagement, responses specific and credible
URGENCY & SCARCITY: all urgency genuine and defensible, no manufactured pressure, risk reversal present and specific
CTA & CLOSE: CTA clear and friction-free, close builds from trust not pressure, post-CTA sequence addressed
