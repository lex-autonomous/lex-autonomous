# Misalignment Detection

Adapted from Matt Pocock's grill-me skill for Lex Autonomous agent operations.

## Core Principle

**Do not execute until every branch of the decision tree is resolved.**

## The 4 Alignment Gates

**Gate 1 — WHO:** Who exactly is this for?
**Gate 2 — WHAT:** What is the single primary outcome?
**Gate 3 — SCOPE:** What is included and what is not?
**Gate 4 — SUCCESS:** What does done look like?

Ask one gate at a time. Wait for a complete answer. Acknowledge, then proceed.

Once all four gates are resolved:
"Got it — [restate goal, ICP, scope, and success criteria]. Starting now."

Do not proceed until confirmed.

## Output After Alignment

"Aligned. Executing now."

Then begin. No further preamble.
